**What was built**

Phase 9.2 added three social primitives to the Family Feed, all automated end-to-end:
- **Reactions (FEED-05):** all 6 allowed emoji in the picker (❤️ 👍 🔥 🏆 😂 🎉) plus a scale-pop
  on the chip whose state just changed. No push is sent for a reaction (D-03).
- **Tease (FEED-06):** a "Подколоть" button on every card except your own, opening a tray of
  pre-written phrases chosen by the card's kind; picking one posts a grape reply bubble under
  the card. One tease per person per card; a second attempt shows "Подколото".
- **Kid→kid medal (FEED-07):** a child on `/kid/feed` sees "🏅 Отправить медаль", picks a
  sibling (when there is more than one) and one of 8 pre-written phrases. The medal carries
  **zero coins** and posts a card titled "Медаль от {имя} 🏅". A child can send one medal per day
  in total; a recipient can still separately receive a parent-sent medal the same day.

Migration `supabase/migrations/2026-09-19-medal-sender.sql` (`medals.sender_role`,
`medals.sender_member_id`, two cap indexes) is applied to prod and re-verified at phase end.

**Verification script** — run against a real family that has **two children**:

1. **Reactions — kid view.** Sign in as a child, open `/kid/feed`. Each card shows six emoji
   chips. Tap 😂: it lights up, the count increments, and the chip visibly pops. Tap again: it
   unlights and pops again. Confirm no push notification arrives on any device.
2. **Reactions — adult view.** Open `/family/feed` (or Parent Center → Лента) as a parent. Same
   six chips; the kid's reaction shows with a count of 1.
3. **Tease — your own card.** As child A, find a card about child A. There is **no** "Подколоть"
   button on it.
4. **Tease — sibling's card.** Find a card about child B. Tap "Подколоть": a phrase tray opens
   below the reaction row. The phrases fit the card type (an achievement card offers
   "Красавчик 🔥" etc.; a purchase card offers "Богач нашёлся 👑" etc.), and there is **no text
   input anywhere**. Tap a phrase: a grape bubble slides in under the card with your name and
   that phrase.
5. **Tease — repeat attempt.** The button now reads "Подколото" and does not reopen the tray.
   Reload: the bubble persists and the button still reads "Подколото".
6. **Tease — the other side.** On child B's device (or the parent feed), the bubble appears, and
   the 💬 comment count on that card did **not** increase.
7. **Kid medal — send.** As child A on `/kid/feed`, tap "🏅 Отправить медаль", pick child B, then
   a phrase. The sheet closes and a new card appears reading "Медаль от {имя A} 🏅" with the
   phrase as its body, a pink/berry left rail, and **no coin figure anywhere on it**.
8. **Kid medal — economy safety.** Open child B's wallet: the coin balance is unchanged and no
   new transaction appeared for the medal.
9. **Kid medal — daily cap.** As child A, tap "🏅 Отправить медаль" again (child B or any other
   sibling). The sheet shows "Ты уже отправил медаль сегодня" instead of the phrase list.
10. **Parent medal alongside.** If a parent medal entry point is in use, send a parent medal to
    child B the same day and confirm it succeeds (the two caps are separate).
11. **Parents see no kid launcher.** As a parent, open `/family/feed` and
    `/kid/feed?preview=true`: "🏅 Отправить медаль" does **not** appear.
12. **Open design question to decide now:** the UI-SPEC's colour table names mango
    (`K.mango`/`K.mangoSoft`/`K.mangoDeep`) for a lit reaction chip, while the shipped chip keeps
    the existing variant accent (sky on kid, indigo on daylight) because the spec's own rationale

    lit chip in both views: keep the current accent, or open a follow-up to recolour the kid
    variant's lit chip to mango?
