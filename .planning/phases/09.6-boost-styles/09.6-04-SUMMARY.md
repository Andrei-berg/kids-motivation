# Phase 09.6 Plan 04: Wire the boost_style picker and thread it into both BoostDetailSheet mounts Summary

**Replaced ProfileSheet's single hardcoded always-active segmented-bar button with a real 3-way `role="radio"` boost_style picker, and passed the child's actual `boost_style` preference into both `BoostDetailSheet` mounts (ProfileSheet's own, and the Day-hero's) — completing BOOST-04/BOOST-05 end to end.**

## Accomplishments

- `ProfileSheet.tsx`'s `handleBoostStyleTap` parameter widened from the literal `'segmented-bar'` to `Child['boost_style']`, mirroring `handleFillStyleTap`'s already-3-way signature — body unchanged

- `app/kid/(app)/day/page.tsx`'s `<BoostDetailSheet>` mount (the Day-hero, full and compact `BoostMeter` entry point) now passes the same `boostStyle={child?.boost_style ?? 'segmented-bar'}`, matching the existing `fillStyle` null-coalescing pattern already used on that page
