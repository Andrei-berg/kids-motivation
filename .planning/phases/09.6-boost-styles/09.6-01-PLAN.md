<objective>
Extend the pure boost view model and its data source with exactly the three things the two new
styles need and today's segmented-bar body does not have: independent (non-collapsed) per-tier grade
state, separate streak×2 / streak×3 state with their own coin amounts, and per-day filled flags for
the 7-dot week strip. Then add every new i18n string both styles render.

Purpose: BOOST-04 needs one row per condition with its own reward and progress (D-01, D-02);
BOOST-05 needs three grade-tier badges plus a next-tier highlight and a week-dot strip (D-06, D-07,
D-09); BOOST-06 requires all of it to derive from `lib/kid/boost-rules.ts`, never from the sketch's
illustrative 150/300/500 and 3/5/7 numbers. Putting this in the pure module makes "no hardcoded
sketch numbers" provable by `npm test` instead of by reading JSX.
Output: an additively-extended `BoostDetailView`, a `filledDayFlags` passthrough in
`getBoostProgress`, new unit tests, and the RU/EN copy. No React and no component in this plan.

Why this is its own plan: both wave-2 body plans consume this contract, and neither can be written
against an unstable view-model shape. Interface-first ordering, same as Phase 9.5's plan 02.
</objective>
