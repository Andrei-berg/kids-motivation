## Accomplishments
- `BoostDetailView.grades` now exposes `tiers: [BoostGradeTier, BoostGradeTier, BoostGradeTier]` (independent `reached`/`threshold`/`current`/`remaining`/`pct`/`coins` per tier) and `nextTierIndex`, computed independently of the pre-existing sequential `tierReached`/`pct` fields the segmented-bar body still uses unchanged
