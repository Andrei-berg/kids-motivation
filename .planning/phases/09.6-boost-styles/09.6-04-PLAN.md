<objective>
Finish wiring `boost_style` end to end: replace `ProfileSheet`'s single hardcoded always-active
`segmented-bar` button with a real 3-option `role="radio"` group (mirroring the `fill_style` picker
directly above it), and thread the resulting preference into `BoostDetailSheet` at both places it is
mounted — `ProfileSheet.tsx` itself and `app/kid/(app)/day/page.tsx` — so a child who picks
quest-checklist or ring-badges actually sees that style, not segmented-bar, no matter which of the 3
tap entry points they use.

Purpose: this is the "Last task: Wire" step of interface-first ordering — plans 09.6-02 and 09.6-03
built both new bodies and the `boostStyle` prop's consumption side; this plan is what makes the
child-facing `boost_style` control real and connects the already-existing preference to the
already-built bodies. Without this plan, BOOST-04/BOOST-05 would be implemented but unreachable from
the UI (no picker offers them, and both `BoostDetailSheet` mounts pass no `boostStyle` prop at all
today).
Output: a working 3-way picker with persisted selection, and both `BoostDetailSheet` mount points
passing the real preference.

Why this waits for all three prior plans: the picker's 3 options are only meaningfully "real" once
both new bodies exist (09.6-02, 09.6-03) and the `boostStyle` prop contract they both consume was
finalized in 09.6-02. Different files than 09.6-02/09.6-03 (`ProfileSheet.tsx` and the day-page
route, not `BoostDetailSheet.tsx`), so this plan could in principle start once the prop shape is
fixed — depending on all three keeps this plan's "own the whole picker" scope honest and matches
this project's own precedent (Phase 9.4's `09.4-04` picker-completion plan depended on both of its
prior style plans, `09.4-02` and `09.4-03`).
</objective>
