One WARNING-level bug was found and independently reproduced: `QuestChecklistBody`'s total
progress bar divides by zero (producing `"NaN%"` CSS) when a family disables the weekly boost
entirely (all three relevant `wallet_settings` coin values set to 0). This is a pre-existing,
narrow, cosmetic-only edge case already caught and documented by code review with a one-line
fix — it does not affect any of the three stated success criteria under normal (non-zeroed)
settings, and is not required to be fixed for this phase's goal to be considered achieved. It
is surfaced here for tracking; recommend a small follow-up fix (mirror `RingBadgesBody`'s
existing `max > 0 ? ... : 0` guard).
