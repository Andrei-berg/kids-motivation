  <what-built>
    The Family Feed is reframed as a recognition stream:
    - A story reel above the card list — one 62px bubble per child who has at least one
      `family_events` row today, showing that child's single highest-ranked highlight
      (badge > medal > boost > streak > reading > reward approved > purchase > day filled).
      A child with no events today gets no bubble at all.
    - Unseen highlight = mango/berry/grape gradient ring; seen = flat neutral `#E9ECF3` border.
      Tapping a bubble opens a full-bleed gradient overlay with the highlight's icon, title and
      body enlarged, and immediately marks it seen for that child for today (localStorage key
      `story_seen_{familyId}_{childId}_{YYYY-MM-DD}`).
    - Every day-grouped card now has a 5px color-coded left rail inside an 18px-radius clipped
      card: mango = boost/streak, berry = medal/badge, mint = day filled, grape = reading/level
      up, sky = purchase/reward approved, neutral = parent note.
    - Every coin amount is gone from every card, for every event kind — purchases show only the
      reward name.
    - Reactions, the comment thread, the parent/extended composer, "Показать ещё" pagination and
      realtime updates are all unchanged and must still work.
  </what-built>

  <how-to-verify>
    Run `npm run dev` and open http://localhost:3000.

    **A. Kid variant — http://localhost:3000/kid/feed** (sign in as a child)
    1. Fill or re-save today's day for one child so at least one `family_events` row exists
       today, then reload the feed.
    2. Confirm a story bubble appears for that child, with a colorful gradient ring, and that
       a child with no events today has NO bubble and no placeholder/"empty" bubble.
    3. Tap the bubble. Confirm a full-screen overlay opens with a colored gradient background,
       a large emoji, a large headline and (if the event has a body) a sub-line.
    4. Confirm the overlay shows **no coin amount** — no `🪙`, no `+N`, no `−N`.
    5. Dismiss it three ways in turn: the × button (top-right), a tap on the dark backdrop, and
       the Escape key. All three must close it.
    6. Confirm the ring is now flat grey. Reload the page — it must still be flat grey.
    7. Scroll the card list: confirm the day labels ("Сегодня" / "Вчера" / a date) still stick
       to the top correctly and do not overlap the story reel.

    **B. Card content and rails — same screen**
    8. Confirm every card shows a thin colored bar down its left edge, and that the color
       matches the event type (a boost/streak card is orange, a badge/medal card is pink, a
       "день заполнен" card is green, a reading card is purple, a purchase/reward card is blue,
       a parent note is pale grey).
    9. Find a reward-purchase card. Confirm it shows only the reward name — **no price, no coin
       figure, no 🪙** anywhere on it.
    10. Scan the entire visible feed for any `🪙` or any `+`/`−` number. There must be none.
    11. Confirm no card shows a penalty, correction, fine, or negative behavior mark.
    12. Tap an emoji reaction on a card — it must toggle on/off with the count updating. Open
        the 💬 comment thread, post a comment, and confirm it appears.

    **C. Family variant — http://localhost:3000/family/feed**
    13. Confirm the same story reel and the same rail colors appear. Specifically: the SAME
        event must show the SAME rail color here as it did on `/kid/feed` (rail colors are
        variant-independent — if they differ between screens, that is a bug).
    14. Confirm the parent/extended composer ("Написать семье…") still posts a note and the
        note card appears with a neutral grey rail.

    **D. Parent Center — http://localhost:3000/parent-center → Feed tab** (sign in as a parent)
    15. Confirm the story reel and rail cards render correctly inside the Parent Center shell
        too, with no layout overflow and no horizontal page scroll.

    **E. Mobile width**
    16. Narrow the browser to ~390px (or use device emulation). Confirm the story reel scrolls
        horizontally without breaking the layout, and the modal fits the screen with its ×
        button reachable.
  </how-to-verify>
